# 01.03 - The product and its audience

**Where the project is now:** The product target is clear. Now define who the app serves so later design and content decisions have a useful filter.

**This step:** Name the visitors: recruiters, collaborators, readers, and the site owner.

## Primary audiences

Recruiters and hiring managers need a fast answer to one question: can this person build credible software? They will scan positioning, projects, links, and contact information quickly.

Technical collaborators need deeper proof. They care about architecture, tradeoffs, source links, diagrams, implementation notes, and whether the work sounds honest rather than inflated.

Readers need articles that are easy to find, open, and trust. Drafts must stay private, published posts need metadata, and article pages should remain readable on small screens.

The site owner is also an audience. You need admin screens that make writing, editing, publishing, and reviewing content safe enough to use after the initial build.

## Product decisions this affects

- The home page should make your role, strengths, and strongest proof visible quickly.
- Project pages should explain problem, solution, implementation, and result.
- Articles should be modeled as content with status and metadata, not loose markdown fragments.
- Admin routes should be protected and boring in the best way: predictable, clear, and hard to misuse.
