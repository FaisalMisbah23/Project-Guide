# 01.03 - The product and its person

The product is a portfolio for a software engineer. The main visitor might be a recruiter, mentor, peer developer, or hiring manager. They are not here to admire a template; they are here to understand your judgment quickly.

Choose a specific identity before coding:

```txt
Portfolio name:
Owner role:
Primary audience:
Domain focus:
Design language:
Signature feature pitch:
```

Example:

```txt
Portfolio name: Signal Stack
Owner role: Full-stack software engineer
Primary audience: startup hiring managers
Domain focus: developer tools
Design language: precise, editorial, high-contrast, minimal motion
Signature feature pitch: An architecture map shows how every service in the app fits together.
```

Use this as a creative constraint. The final app is still a software engineer portfolio.
