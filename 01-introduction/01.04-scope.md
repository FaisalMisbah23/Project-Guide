# 01.04 - Scope

**Where the project is now:** You know the product and its audiences. Now draw the boundary around what belongs in this course.

**This step:** Separate the portfolio, publishing workflow, contact channel, and deployment from nice-to-have extras.

## In scope

The course builds a portfolio app with a public site, project catalogue, article system, admin authentication, dashboard, editor workflow, revision history, contact form, security checks, SEO basics, and deployment.

That is enough surface area to practice real application decisions without losing the shape of the project.

## Out of scope

You will not build a full CMS, a multi-user SaaS admin, payments, comments, notifications, rich text collaboration, custom analytics dashboards, or a design system meant for many products.

Those are valid future improvements, but adding them now would blur the course goal. The first win is a complete, maintainable portfolio with a clear owner workflow.

## How to handle ideas that appear mid-course

Write tempting extras in a `docs/future-improvements.md` file once the project exists. Do not let them interrupt the current chapter unless they fix a real bug or unblock the required outcome.

## Continue when

You can name what the course will build, what it deliberately avoids, and where future ideas should go.
