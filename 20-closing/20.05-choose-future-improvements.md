# 20.05 - Choose Future Improvements

The build is finished enough to prove. This step turns the project into something you can demo, explain, and improve without reopening every decision.

The weak approach is to keep adding features because another feature feels easier than calling v1 done. The professional approach is to freeze the core, rehearse the story, write the trade-offs, and choose a small backlog.

✅ Do connect every claim in your demo to a route, table, function, or deployed behavior. ❌ Don't rely on screenshots when you can show the working app.

Write the final v2 ideas in `docs/future-backlog.md`, then summarize the trade-off in `learning-log/20-closing.md`.
